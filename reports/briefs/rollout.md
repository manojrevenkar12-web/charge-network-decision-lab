# Where the next 1,000 points go

**Which sites, and how many points each, for next year's 1,000 high-power points?**

Build 107 sites with 1044 points (IT 35, GB 34, DE 29, AT 5, PL 4 sites), for €188m: expected ten-year NPV €519m, and €474m in the worst tenth of scenarios.

## Decision

Approve this plan. Choosing by traffic alone is expected to earn €171m less; ranking by expected NPV without looking at sites that compete with each other, €22m less.

## What this rests on

- Demand from a Bayesian hierarchical model of 637 established sites (NUTS, r-hat 1.01); unexplained site-to-site variation is 0.45 on the log scale, which is most of the risk.
- A nearby charging site costs demand (coefficient -0.14 per unit of proximity); the plan charges for new sites that would compete with each other.
- New sites priced like the 2025 increase, with its effect from question 1, and grown with question 4's country growth.
- 1400 candidates, sizes of 4, 8 or 12 points, within the budget, with at least 18 Italian sites and at most a third of the points in one country.

## What would change it

- A different capex or energy price, which moves sizes before it moves sites.
- Grid connection quotes that differ from the planning figures.

## Method

NumPyro hierarchical model, posterior predictive scenarios, ten-year NPV per scenario, mixed-integer programme with CVaR (HiGHS).
