# The night discount trial

**Should the night discount go network-wide?**

The 30% night discount moves 4.2 points of energy to the night and cuts daytime sessions by 6%, but costs €143 of revenue per site and day.

## Decision

Do not roll it out at 30%. The relieved daytime demand is worth less than the revenue it gives away; test a smaller discount on the busiest sites only.

## What this rests on

- 80 sites, 4480 site-days, discount assigned by coin flip each day (randomisation p = 0.002).
- Designed from pre-trial data: the trial could detect €32 a day (1.5% of revenue) with 80% power.
- Yesterday's discount still shifts a little charging today (0.4 points), so the model separates the two.
- An always-valid sequential test would have stopped the trial on day 7.

## What would change it

- A higher value of a relieved daytime session than €4 (grid peak charges, queues), which would have to exceed about €31 to pay.

## Method

Switchback design by site and day; fixed effects for site and date with yesterday's assignment; clustered standard errors; randomisation inference; CUPED for design; mixture SPRT.
