# A competitor next door

**What does a competitor's hub next to our site cost?**

At AT-0201, a competitor hub opened on 2025-11-29 took about 16% of sessions. Across all 131 openings in the history the loss is 14%.

## Decision

Price the risk into site choices: a new site next to an existing charger should expect to lose about this share, which the rollout model includes.

## What this rests on

- A synthetic control from 75 motorway high-power sites without a competitor or a price change, matching the site's path before the opening (pre-period error 7.2%).
- Placebo test: the gap is larger than at 95% of donor sites where nothing happened (p = 0.05).
- Openings fall on dates nobody at EDRI chose, so the network-wide estimate can assume parallel trends.

## What would change it

- A second competitor or a price war nearby, which the placebo sites would not reflect.

## Method

Synthetic control with an intercept and placebo inference; network-wide imputation estimator with site bootstrap.
